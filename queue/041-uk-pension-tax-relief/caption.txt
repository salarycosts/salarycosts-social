What a UK pension contribution really costs.

On a £50,000 salary, paying £5,000 into a pension lowers take-home pay by about £4,000, because tax relief covers the rest. The pot still grows by the full £5,000.

General information, not tax advice. 2026/27 rules, England, tax relief at the basic rate; schemes differ.
