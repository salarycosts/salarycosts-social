How big should an emergency fund be?

A common starting point is three to six months of expenses. Using take-home pay as an upper limit, a $60,000 salary in Texas gives $4,199 a month, so three months is $12,598 and six months is $25,195.

Single filer in Texas, 2026 rules. Your own spending is the better benchmark.
