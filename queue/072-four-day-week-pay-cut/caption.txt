Working four days instead of five: what changes on your payslip?

Moving from $80,000 to $64,000 is a 20% pay cut, but take-home pay falls by only 15.9% to 18.7% depending on the country, because the pay you give up was taxed at your highest rate.

Single employee, 2026 rules (US: Texas), converted at ECB rates of 2 October 2026.
