How to use the two three-paycheck months.

If you are paid every two weeks, you receive 26 paychecks a year, not 24. On a $75,000 salary in Texas, each paycheck is about $2,369, and two months a year bring a third one.

Single filer in Texas, 2026 rules, before any benefit deductions.
