What an hourly wage means per month.

At 40 hours a week, $15 an hour is $31,200 a year and about $2,271 a month after tax. $30 an hour is $62,400 a year and $4,360 a month.

Single filer in Texas, 2,080 working hours a year, 2026 federal and payroll taxes.
