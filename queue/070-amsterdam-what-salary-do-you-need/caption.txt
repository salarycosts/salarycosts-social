What salary do you need to live in Amsterdam?

A month for one adult in a one-bedroom costs about €2,450 in Amsterdam. To cover that and still save 20% of take-home pay, you would need roughly €45,000 gross a year. Sharing a home brings the figure down to about €33,500; a two-bedroom pushes it to €65,500.
