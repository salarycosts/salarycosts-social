What a 5% raise is really worth.

A raise from $60,000 to $63,000 adds $1,561 a year to take-home pay in Germany and $2,411 in the USA. Same gross raise, very different result.

Single employee, 2026 rules (US: Texas), salary converted at ECB rates of 2 October 2026.
