The 50/30/20 rule, in actual dollars.

On a $60,000 salary in Texas, take-home pay is about $4,199 a month. Under the rule, that is $2,100 for needs, $1,260 for wants and $840 for saving or paying off debt.

A guideline, not a law: adjust to your cost of living. Single filer, 2026 rules.

Work out your own number: salarycosts.com (link in bio)
