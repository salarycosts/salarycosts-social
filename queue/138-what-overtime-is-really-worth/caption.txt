What overtime is really worth after tax.

On a $50,000 salary in Texas, 10 hours of overtime a week at time-and-a-half adds about $18,029 gross over 50 weeks. After federal and payroll taxes you keep roughly $14,333, or $29 for every extra hour.

Single filer, 2026 rules, overtime paid at 1.5 times a rate of salary over 2,080 hours.
