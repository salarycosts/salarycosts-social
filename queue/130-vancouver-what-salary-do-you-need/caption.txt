What salary do you need to live in Vancouver?

A month for one adult in a one-bedroom costs about $3,427 in Vancouver. To cover that and still save 20% of take-home pay, you would need roughly $65,500 gross a year. Sharing a home brings the figure down to about $46,000; a two-bedroom pushes it to $83,000.
