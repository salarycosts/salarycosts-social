The UK tax trap between £100,000 and £125,000.

On a salary of £110,000, you keep roughly 38p of every extra pound. Go from £100,000 to £125,000 and take-home pay rises by only £9,500, because the personal allowance is withdrawn.

General information, not tax advice. England, 2026/27 rules, single employee, default settings.
