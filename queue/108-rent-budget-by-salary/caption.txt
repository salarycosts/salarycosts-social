A rent budget that starts from take-home pay.

On a $60,000 salary in Texas, take-home is about $4,199 a month. A 30% rent ceiling is $1,260; a stricter 25% is $1,050.

A guideline, not a rule. Single filer in Texas, 2026 rules.

Work out your own number: salarycosts.com (link in bio)
