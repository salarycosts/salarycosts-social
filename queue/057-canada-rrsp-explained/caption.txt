What a C$5,000 RRSP contribution does to your tax bill.

On C$80,000 in Ontario, a C$5,000 RRSP contribution lowers your tax bill by about $1,483. It costs you roughly $3,518 in cash flow for $5,000 in the account.

General information, not tax advice. Single filer, 2026 rules; contribution room differs for everyone.
