What are FICA, Social Security and Medicare? 🧾

Social Security is 6.2% of wages up to $184,500 and Medicare is 1.45% of all wages (plus 0.9% above $200,000). On $100,000 that's $7,650 a year, charged on top of federal and state income tax. Your employer pays a matching amount.
