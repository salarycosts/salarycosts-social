How long does it take to save $10,000?

Saving $250 a month takes 40 months, $500 takes 20 months and $1,000 takes 10 months, before any interest. On a $60,000 salary in Texas, $500 is 12% of monthly take-home.

Savings only, no interest assumed. Single filer in Texas, 2026 rules.
