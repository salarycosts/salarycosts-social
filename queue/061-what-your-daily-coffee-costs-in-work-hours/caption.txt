What a daily coffee costs in hours of work.

On a $60,000 salary in Texas, take-home pay is about $24 for every hour worked. A $5 coffee on all 260 workdays costs $1,300, which equals roughly 54 hours of your take-home pay.

Single filer, 2,080 working hours a year, 2026 rules.
