What salary do you need to live in Barcelona?

A month for one adult in a one-bedroom costs about €2,128 in Barcelona. To cover that and still save 20% of take-home pay, you would need roughly €44,000 gross a year. Sharing a home brings the figure down to about €29,500; a two-bedroom pushes it to €54,500.
