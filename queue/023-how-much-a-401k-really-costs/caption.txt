A $10,000 401(k) contribution rarely costs $10,000.

On a $100,000 salary, putting $10,000 into a traditional 401(k) cuts federal income tax by about $2,200. Your take-home pay falls by roughly $7,800, while the account grows by the full $10,000.

General information, not tax advice. Single filer, 2026 federal rules; contribution limits change every year.
