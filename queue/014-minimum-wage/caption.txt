What a full-time minimum wage is actually worth.

At 40 hours a week, the statutory minimum wage works out to $37,330 a year in Luxembourg and $13,392 in Malta, converted to US dollars. Countries without a national minimum are not included.

Annual gross, 52 weeks. Exchange rates: ECB, 2026-10-02.
