The two payroll taxes behind most US paychecks.

Social Security takes 6.2% of wages up to $184,500, and Medicare takes 1.45% of all wages, plus 0.9% above $200,000. On $100,000 that comes to $7,650 a year, before federal and state income tax.

Employee share only. Your employer pays a matching amount on top.

Work out your own number: salarycosts.com (link in bio)
