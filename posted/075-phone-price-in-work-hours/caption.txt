How many hours of work does a new phone cost?

On a $50,000 salary, take-home is $16 an hour in Germany and $20 an hour in the USA. A $1,200 phone therefore takes 76 and 59 hours of work.

Single employee, 2,080 working hours, 2026 rules (US: Texas), converted at ECB rates of 2 October 2026.
