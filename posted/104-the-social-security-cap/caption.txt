Social Security tax stops at a cap, and that changes the maths for high earners.

Social Security takes 6.2% of wages up to $184,500 in 2026. On $500,000 of wages that is $11,439, or 2.3% of pay, compared with 6.2% on $100,000.

Employee share only, 2026 rules. The wage base is adjusted every year.
