Tennessee does not tax wages, and it shows on a $75,000 paycheck.

On $75,000 in Tennessee, take-home pay is about $61,593 a year, or $5,133 a month. There is no state income tax on wages there, so only federal income tax and payroll taxes come out. Out of 51 states and D.C., Tennessee ranks #1 (tied with 6 others).
