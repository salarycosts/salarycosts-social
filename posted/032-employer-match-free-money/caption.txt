If your employer matches 401(k) contributions, this is the maths.

On a $75,000 salary, a 4% contribution is $3,000 a year. After the federal tax saving it costs you about $2,340 in take-home pay, and a 4% match adds another $3,000. That puts $6,000 into the account.

Illustration with a 4% match; your plan may differ. Single filer, 2026 federal rules.
