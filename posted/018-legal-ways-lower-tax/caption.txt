Three common ways employees legally reduce their tax.

On a $100,000 US salary, $10,000 into a traditional 401(k) cuts federal tax by $2,200. On C$80,000 in Ontario, C$5,000 into an RRSP saves $1,483. On a 50,000 pound UK salary, 5,000 pounds into a pension through salary sacrifice saves £1,000.

General information, not tax advice. Limits change every year.
