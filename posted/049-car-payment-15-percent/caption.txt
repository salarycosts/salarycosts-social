A car budget that starts from your take-home pay.

On a $75,000 salary in Texas, take-home is about $5,133 a month, so 15% is $770 for the payment, insurance, fuel and maintenance together. A $600 payment alone is 17% of monthly take-home at $50,000.

A guideline, not advice. Single filer in Texas, 2026 rules.

Work out your own number: salarycosts.com (link in bio)
