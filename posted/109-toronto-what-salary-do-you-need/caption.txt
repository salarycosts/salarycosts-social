What salary do you need to live in Toronto?

A month for one adult in a one-bedroom costs about $3,506 in Toronto. To cover that and still save 20% of take-home pay, you would need roughly $68,000 gross a year. Sharing a home brings the figure down to about $45,000; a two-bedroom pushes it to $85,000.
