What salary do you need to live in Cork?

A month for one adult in a one-bedroom costs about €2,728 in Cork. To cover that and still save 20% of take-home pay, you would need roughly €52,500 gross a year. Sharing a home brings the figure down to about €30,500; a two-bedroom pushes it to €66,500.
