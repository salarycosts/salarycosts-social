What a $75,000 salary leaves you in Utah.

On $75,000 in Utah, take-home pay is about $58,483 a year, or $4,874 a month. That is $3,110 less than the same salary leaves in Texas, which has no state income tax. Out of 51 states and D.C., Utah ranks #37.
