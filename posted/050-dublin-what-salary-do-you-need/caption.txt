What salary do you need to live in Dublin?

A month for one adult in a one-bedroom costs about €3,128 in Dublin. To cover that and still save 20% of take-home pay, you would need roughly €64,000 gross a year. Sharing a home brings the figure down to about €36,500; a two-bedroom pushes it to €79,000.
